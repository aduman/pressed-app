# Pressed

Marketing site, support, and privacy policy for **Pressed**, an on-device risograph-style and screen-print photo app for iOS.

- [Landing page](https://aduman.github.io/pressed-app/)
- [日本語 / press page](https://aduman.github.io/pressed-app/ja/)
- [Privacy Policy](https://aduman.github.io/pressed-app/privacy.html)
- [Support](https://aduman.github.io/pressed-app/support.html)

`press/pressed-press-kit.zip` is generated, not hand-assembled. It is rebuilt by
`store/tools/make_press_kit.py` in the app repo, which records what goes in and what is
deliberately left out.
