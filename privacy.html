Privacy Policy

Pressed

Last updated 21 June 2026

Pressed does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

All editing happens locally on your iPhone or iPad. The photos you open and the prints you create never leave your device. Your saved presses and settings are stored only on your device.

Photo library

Pressed asks for permission to add photos to your library only so it can save a print you export. It does not read or upload your photo library. You choose each photo to open through the system photo picker.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Pressed Plus" unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Pressed never sees your payment information. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Data collection

None. Pressed collects no usage data and contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, or trackers. This matches the "Data Not Collected" label on the App Store.

Children

Pressed is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
