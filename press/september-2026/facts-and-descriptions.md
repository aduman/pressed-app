# Pressed — facts and editorial copy

Checked 11 September 2026.

## One sentence

Pressed is a pocket print studio that turns your photos into expressive artwork through ink, paper and halftone.

## Short description

Give a familiar photo a different character. Pressed lets you choose the inks, shape the halftone and explore the texture of paper on iPhone and iPad. Start with a ready-made press, then refine the image until it feels like your own. Download free, with watermark-free exports and an optional one-time Plus purchase.

## Extended description

Pressed brings the creative decisions of print to your photographs. Choose a palette, explore how the inks overlap, then refine the dots, lines, paper and registration. A bright snapshot can become a bold graphic; a flower can become a quiet single-ink study. Hold the preview to compare your work with the original.

Pressed 2.0 introduces Quick Press, Test Strips and Ink Match to help you explore a direction. Press Sets keeps a series together while letting you refine individual images. Photo processing happens on your device.

The app is free to download and exports images without a watermark. Pressed Plus is a one-time purchase that expands export options and creative tools, including custom inks, transparent stickers, PDF separations and Press Run video.

## Free and Plus

Free includes the introductory presses, image adjustments, watermark-free image export and creating/preparing Press Sets. Plus adds selected premium presses, custom ink export, larger image export, transparent die-cut stickers with a white border, unlimited saved presses, PDF separation export, Press Set package export and Press Run video.

## Useful editorial details

- The artwork is digital. Physical printing, page layout and finishing are separate steps.
- The PDF separation export contains a grayscale page per ink with fixed crop and registration marks. A receiving printer should confirm page size, screening and production requirements.
- The custom recipes in this pack demonstrate settings available in Pressed; they are named for this study and are not claims about bundled preset names.
- The 24-second film is an edited demonstration of source images and actual exports. It is not a real-time recording of app processing speed.
- Pressed’s Plus purchase can be tested independently through the published app. No promo code or special review entitlement is included in this pack.

## Maker bio

Ahmet Duman is the independent developer of Pressed, a creative app for iPhone and iPad. His work on Pressed brings ink selection, colour separation, paper and halftone into a photographic editing workflow.

## Three editorial angles

1. **Apple / app discovery:** a small independent app with a tangible, highly visual creative workflow and a one-time upgrade.
2. **Design / creative practice:** the same source material can take on very different identities through deliberate ink, screen and paper choices.
3. **Photography:** original/result comparisons make the transformation inspectable, while downloadable recipes invite readers to try it with their own photographs.
