## File notes

The six artwork JPEGs are RGB, between 2,731 and 3,936 pixels wide. Separate thumbnails are for fast browsing; use the files under `images/` for publication. The PNG interface images are native captures. Recipe files contain the settings used for each print; they are custom study recipes.

The transformation film is silent, with baked-in English titles. It is an edited presentation of actual exports, not a timing benchmark or a physical printing demonstration. The closing credit identifies the source photographers.

## Use

These materials are supplied for editorial coverage of Pressed and the Second Impressions study. You may resize or crop them to fit your article or post.
