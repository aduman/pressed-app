# A short visit to Pressed

Start with a photograph of your own. An image with a clear subject and several areas of colour makes the print choices easy to inspect.

1. **Find a direction.** Open the photo and explore the available presses. Quick Press, Test Strips and Ink Match provide additional ways to explore in 2.0.
2. **Look at what changed.** Hold the preview to compare the result with the original. Notice which areas carry ink and which remain paper.
3. **Make one deliberate change.** Try another dot shape or adjust dot size and ink level. Then explore a different paper or screen angle.
4. **Save a result.** Export a free image and inspect the full-size file. Free image exports have no watermark.
5. **Explore a collection.** Create a Press Set with several images. You can prepare the set for free; its package export belongs to Plus.

## The recipes in this pack

Each `.pressed` file preserves a custom study’s settings. Open/share it to Pressed through the system’s file-opening options. The files contain recipe settings, not the photographer’s image; choose your own image in the app. Custom ink export and some other tools require Plus. The app also supports one free export of a shared recipe; use the app’s current entitlement messages as the guide for your session.

The supplied artwork demonstrates the recipe and can be compared against the source image in the gallery. Different source colours, detail and resolution will produce a different result with the same recipe.

## If your article goes into printing

Pressed supplies digital images and generic per-ink PDF separations. A physical printer should check the exported page size, screening and production requirements before use. Printing, imposition and page-layout work are separate from the app.

For an interview, a particular file format or a question while testing: press@almostmatter.com.
