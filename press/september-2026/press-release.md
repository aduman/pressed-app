# Pressed 2.0 gives your photos a new life in ink

An independent creative app for iPhone and iPad brings ink, paper and halftone into a hands-on photographic workflow.

11 September 2026 — Pressed 2.0 is available on the App Store, with new ways to explore a photograph, compare print treatments and develop a related series of images.

Pressed turns a photograph into digital artwork built from overlapping ink colours, visible screens and textured paper. A change in palette can make a familiar subject feel entirely different: a still life becomes a vivid graphic, flowers take on the softness of a botanical study, and a coastal scene becomes a tightly drawn print. The original image remains the starting point throughout.

Version 2.0 adds Quick Press, Test Strips and Ink Match to help people find a direction. Press Sets brings several photographs into one project, with room to refine each image. The editor offers control over the crop, framing, ink balance, screen angle, dot size and shape, paper texture, dot gain and registration. Pressed’s True Color separation mode explores how selected inks combine on the chosen paper.

The accompanying *Second Impressions* study presents three credited photographs through six individually composed presses. Each treatment has its own ink palette, paper and screen character. Full-resolution artwork, original/result comparisons, downloadable recipes and English interface images are available in the media pack.

Pressed is free to download, with watermark-free image exports up to 4,096 pixels on the long edge. Pressed Plus is a one-time US$9.99 purchase in the US storefront. It adds selected premium looks, custom ink export, exports up to 6,000 pixels, transparent stickers, unlimited saved presses, PDF separations, Press Set package export and Press Run video. Photo processing takes place on the device.
