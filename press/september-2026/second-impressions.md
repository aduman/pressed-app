# Second Impressions

Three photographs. Six individual presses. A study in seeing the everyday again.

An orange, a vase of tulips and an empty lifeguard tower form the starting points for *Second Impressions*, a digital print study made with Pressed. The project explores how much of an image’s identity comes from the way it is printed: which colours are allowed in, which areas belong to the paper, and how the screen describes a surface.

Each work has its own press. *Citrus Circuit* preserves the sharp colour relationships of the original through three inks and a fine round screen. *Acid Cut* pushes those same circles towards a louder graphic language, with a two-colour palette and large square dots. *Rose and Leaf* uses soft botanical colour and elliptical dots; *Botanical No. 2* reduces the tulips to a single burgundy ink and stippled paper. *Signal* gives the coastal scene a fine linear structure. *Night Watch* reinterprets it in dark teal, black and coarse crosshatching on manila.

The photographs remain recognisable, but the atmosphere changes. The six works are presented with the source images, finished exports and exact recipes, making each interpretation open to inspection and reuse within Pressed.

The collection is a completed digital project. The images are exports, with editorial page design applied separately where shown. No physical edition or printer collaboration is claimed.
