One paragraph
-------------

Pressed turns a photo into a printed sheet on an iPhone or iPad. It separates the image
into spot inks, screens each ink as its own halftone at its own angle, overprints them so
two inks mix into a believable colour, and lets the paper show through. Because it follows
the order a press follows, registration drift, ink bleed and dot gain survive into the
result instead of being painted on. Everything runs on the device. No generative AI, no
account, no cloud. It exports print-ready per-ink PDF separations with registration marks
and die-cut stickers, so the output is a thing you can actually print.
