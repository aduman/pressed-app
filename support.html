Frequently asked

How do I make a print?

Open a photo, tap a press from the strip, then fine-tune with Adjust (dot size, registration, bleed, paper). Hold the image to compare with the original, then Export.

What is in Pressed Plus?

A one-time purchase that unlocks every press, all inks and papers, the custom palette editor, unlimited saved presses, die-cut sticker export, and print-ready per-ink PDF separations. No subscription.

What are the PDF separations for?

If you print on a real risograph or screen-printing setup, the separations export gives each ink as its own page with crop and registration marks, ready to send to a printer.

Do my photos leave my device?

Pressed processes photos locally and does not upload them to our servers. You choose when to save or share exports. Optional announcements send notification identifiers and preferences, not your photos. See the privacy policy.

I bought Plus on another device.

Open Settings inside Pressed and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is tied to your Apple ID.
