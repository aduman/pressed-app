Most print filters lay a flat duotone over your photo. Pressed builds the print the way a real press does: it separates the image into spot inks, screens each ink as its own halftone, overprints them so two inks mix into a believable color, and lets the paper show through.

Made for zine makers, sticker and poster artists, and anyone who loves the look of real ink on paper.

What it does

  • A library of curated presses: one to four inks on real paper stocks.
  • Authentic print physics: per-ink halftone screens, registration drift, ink bleed, and dot gain.
  • Color separation that routes each ink by hue, for a true color reduction.
  • Plus: a custom palette editor, saved presses, die-cut sticker export, and print-ready per-ink PDF separations with registration marks.
